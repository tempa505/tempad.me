Tempa Dorji

A computer science student and creative builder drawn to the space where logic meets art, turning ideas into experiences through code, design, and storytelling.

EDUCATION & BACKGROUND

CS major with a Data Science minor. Proud alumna of UWC Red Cross Nordic in Norway, where I learned that fjords are beautiful, but winter darkness is very real.

I come from a small village in Bhutan, where the internet arrives fashionably late.

Growing up there, technology felt distant, almost mythical. Prayer flags moved in mountain winds, and the seasons set the pace of life. That contrast is what fuels me now, bridging the quiet wisdom of my roots with the buzzing possibilities of the digital age.

It is a strange and wonderful thing to come from a place where community is everything, and now to be building in a world where a line of code can reach millions.
